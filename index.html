How this works

Delivered flow is estimated with the standard hydraulic ram pump relation:

q = e × Q × H ÷ h

where Q is supply flow, H is drive head, h is delivery head, and e is pump efficiency (D’Aubuisson definition, e = qh ÷ QH). Efficiency is auto-suggested from published h/H performance data for well-tuned single-stage rams (roughly 75% at low ratios, tapering off as the ratio climbs past ~30); actual efficiency varies by pump model and installation quality, so treat results as an estimate for first-pass sizing, not a guarantee.
